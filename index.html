Tap to begin or continue dialogue. Tap the left or right quarter to move left or right. In the middle half, tap the upper half for up or lower half for down. Swipe movement is also supported. Hold for a hint. Hold the attempt counter to reveal monsters on the first large random bridge. Reveal is disabled on the last adaptive bridge. Keyboard: arrows or WASD to move, Enter or Space to continue, Escape for a hint, R to reveal monsters on the random bridge.
